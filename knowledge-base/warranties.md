# Warranties

## Overview
EcoHome provides layered warranty coverage across all products and installations. There are three distinct types of warranty you should be aware of: **product warranty**, **performance guarantee**, and **workmanship warranty**.

---

| Panels (performance) | 25 years | Minimum 80% output guaranteed at year 25 |

## Workmanship Warranty
All EcoHome installations carry a **minimum 2-year workmanship warranty** covering defects in the installation itself (not the product). This is a requirement of our RECC (Renewable Energy Consumer Code) membership.

In practice, EcoHome provides:
- **Solar installations:** 10-year workmanship warranty
- **Battery installations:** 5-year workmanship warranty
- **EV charger installations:** 3-year workmanship warranty
- **Thermostat installations:** 2-year workmanship warranty

---

## What Happens if EcoHome Ceases Trading?
EcoHome is a member of **RECC (Renewable Energy Consumer Code)** and **HIES (Home Insulation & Energy Systems Quality Assured Contractors Scheme)**. These schemes provide:
- Insurance-backed guarantee protection — your workmanship warranty remains valid even if EcoHome ceases to trade
- A complaints and resolution process independent of EcoHome
- Access to an ombudsman if disputes cannot be resolved directly

---

## MCS Certificate
All EcoHome solar and battery installations come with an **MCS (Microgeneration Certification Scheme) certificate**. This is important because:
- It is required to register for the Smart Export Guarantee (SEG) — without it you cannot be paid for energy exported to the grid
- It validates your product and workmanship warranties with insurers
- It is required for some government grant schemes (ECO4, Warm Homes)
- It is transferable if you sell your home — the new owner benefits from the remaining warranty

Your MCS certificate is issued 2–4 weeks after installation once paperwork is filed with the MCS register.

## What Voids Your Warranty
The following actions will void product and workmanship warranties:
- Modification or repair by an unqualified third party
- Physical damage caused by misuse, vandalism, or force majeure not covered by home insurance
- Installation by a non-MCS-certified engineer (for solar/battery)
- Failure to register the product within 30 days of installation (where required)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**My inverter has failed outside its 5-year warranty — what can I do?**
Inverter extended warranties (up to 12 years) can be purchased at point of sale or within the first year of installation. After the warranty period, EcoHome offers a fixed-price inverter replacement service — contact our support team for a quote.

**Does the solar panel warranty cover storm damage?**
No. Physical damage from storms, hail, or impact is a home insurance matter. EcoHome recommends informing your home insurer that solar panels have been installed, as cover requirements may vary.

**Can I transfer my warranty to a new homeowner?**
Yes. Product warranties are tied to the installation address. The MCS certificate and RECC guarantee transfer automatically. Inform EcoHome in writing when a property is sold so records can be updated.

**Does DIY maintenance affect my warranty?**
Cleaning the panels yourself (with water) is fine. Any electrical work or physical modification must be carried out by a qualified engineer to preserve the warranty.
