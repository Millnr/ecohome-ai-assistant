# Smart Thermostats

## Overview
EcoHome's smart thermostats give you precise control over your home heating — reducing energy waste, lowering bills, and integrating with your solar and battery system for whole-home energy optimisation.

## Product Range

### EcoHome ThermIQ Basic
- **Type:** Scheduling thermostat with remote app control
- **Compatibility:** Most gas boilers, oil boilers, and electric heating systems
- **Features:** 7-day schedule, remote on/off via app, real-time temperature display
- **Installation:** Professional fit recommended; some DIY-capable users can self-install
- **Warranty:** 2 years

### EcoHome ThermIQ Smart
- **Type:** Learning thermostat
- **Compatibility:** Most gas, oil, and electric systems; compatible with heat pumps (confirm model at survey)
- **Features:** Learns your routine after 1 week, auto-adjusts for weather, presence detection via phone GPS, hot water control, TRV integration
- **Installation:** Professional installation included in price
- **Warranty:** 3 years

### EcoHome ThermIQ Pro
- **Type:** Multi-zone learning thermostat with full solar/battery integration
- **Compatibility:** Gas, oil, heat pumps, underfloor heating, hybrid systems
- **Features:** All ThermIQ Smart features plus: room-by-room zone control (up to 8 zones), solar surplus heating mode, EV charging coordination, energy dashboard
- **Installation:** Professional installation required — typically half day
- **Warranty:** 3 years + 2 years extended available

## Typical Savings
Upgrading from a basic programmer to a smart thermostat typically saves **£75–£150 per year** on heating bills (Energy Saving Trust estimate). Savings vary by property size, existing controls, and usage habits.

## Key Features
- **Presence detection:** Detects when you leave home and reduces heating automatically
- **Weather compensation:** Adjusts target temperature based on outside temperature
- **Solar surplus mode (ThermIQ Pro):** Diverts excess solar generation to heating hot water or boosting temperature rather than exporting at low SEG rates
- **TRV integration:** Pairs with smart thermostatic radiator valves for room-by-room control
- **Voice assistant compatible:** Works with Amazon Alexa, Google Home, Apple HomeKit
- **EcoHome app:** Unified dashboard with solar, battery, EV, and heating data

## Compatibility
- **Gas boilers:** Compatible with most combi, system, and regular (heat only) boilers
- **Heat pumps:** ThermIQ Smart and Pro are heat-pump compatible — confirm at survey as some older heat pump models require specific wiring
- **Underfloor heating:** ThermIQ Pro with underfloor heating controller (sold separately)
- **Electric storage heaters:** Not compatible with current range — check back for future products

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Will a smart thermostat work with my boiler?**
Most modern gas and oil boilers are compatible. Our survey will confirm compatibility before installation. Heat pump compatibility is model-dependent.

**Can I install it myself?**
ThermIQ Basic can be self-installed by confident DIYers — a wiring guide is included. ThermIQ Smart and Pro require professional installation for warranty purposes.

**How long does installation take?**
Typically 1–2 hours for a standard single-zone install. Multi-zone ThermIQ Pro installs take 3–4 hours.

**Does it work when I'm away from home?**
Yes. Full remote control is available anywhere with a phone signal via the EcoHome app.

**Will it reduce my bills if I already have a programmer?**
Very likely. A basic time-programmer doesn't adapt to your actual behaviour or outside temperature. A learning thermostat optimises heating in ways a fixed schedule cannot.
