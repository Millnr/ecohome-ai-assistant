## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do solar panels work on cloudy days?**
Yes. Panels generate electricity from daylight, not direct sunlight. Output is reduced on heavily overcast days but panels still produce power year-round in the UK.

**What direction should my roof face?**
South-facing roofs yield the highest output. South-west and south-east orientations are also effective. North-facing roofs are generally not recommended.

**What roof angle is ideal?**
A pitch of 30–40 degrees is optimal. EcoHome can install on roofs from 15 to 50 degrees.

**Will my home need any electrical upgrades?**
Our surveyors assess your existing consumer unit during the free survey. Most modern homes require no upgrades.

**Can I sell excess electricity back to the grid?**
Yes. You can register with the Smart Export Guarantee (SEG) through your energy supplier to receive payments for surplus energy exported to the grid.

## Safety & Compliance
All EcoHome solar installations comply with:
- MCS (Microgeneration Certification Scheme) standards
- BS 7671 IET Wiring Regulations
- Planning permission requirements (most domestic installs are permitted development)

> **Important:** EcoHome solar panels must be installed by a qualified MCS-certified engineer. DIY installation voids the product warranty and may breach building regulations.
