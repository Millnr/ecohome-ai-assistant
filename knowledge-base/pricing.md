# Pricing

## Overview
All prices below are indicative guides based on typical UK market rates as of 2025. Actual quotes depend on your property, system size, roof type, and any additional works required. All prices shown **include 0% VAT** (in effect until March 2027).

> EcoHome provides a free no-obligation quote. Use this document for ballpark guidance only — never quote a customer a fixed price without a formal survey.

---

## Solar Panels

| System | Typical Price (incl. 0% VAT) | Suitable For |
|---|---|---|
| 3 kW Starter | £5,000 – £6,500 | 1–2 bed homes |
| 5 kW Plus | £7,000 – £9,000 | 3–4 bed homes |
| 8 kW Max | £10,000 – £13,000 | Large homes / high users |

- Scaffold costs are typically included in the above
- South-facing roof, good condition — expect the lower end of range
- Complex roof, additional works — expect the higher end

**Payback period:** Typically **8–12 years** depending on energy usage, SEG export rate, and tariff. After payback, generation is essentially free.

**Annual savings:** A typical 5 kW system can save £600–£1,000/year in electricity bills (varies by usage, tariff, and amount self-consumed vs exported).

## Home Batteries

| Unit | Capacity | Typical Price (incl. 0% VAT) |
|---|---|---|
| PowerVault 5 | 5 kWh | £3,500 – £4,500 |
| PowerVault 10 | 10 kWh | £5,500 – £7,000 |
| PowerVault 15 | 15 kWh | £7,500 – £9,500 |

- Prices above are for battery-only installation
- Combined **solar + battery packages** typically save £500–£1,500 vs buying separately
- Typical combined solar + battery (5 kW solar + 10 kWh battery): **£11,000 – £15,000**

**Payback period:** Battery-only: **7–12 years**. Battery paired with solar: improves solar payback by 1–3 years through higher self-consumption.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why do quotes vary so much?**
Price depends on roof complexity, required scaffolding, consumer unit condition, cabling distance, and local installer labour rates. Always get 3 quotes.

**Is VAT really 0%?**
Yes — solar panels, batteries, heat pumps, EV chargers, and associated installation labour are charged at 0% VAT until at least March 2027. This is applied automatically in all EcoHome quotes.

**Can I pay in instalments?**
EcoHome offers finance options via a third-party provider. Details are provided at quotation stage. The Warm Homes low-interest loan is also an option for eligible customers.

**Will prices go up?**
Panel and battery component prices have generally trended downward over time. However, labour costs and supply chain factors can cause short-term variation. A confirmed EcoHome quote is valid for 30 days.
