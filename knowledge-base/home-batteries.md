# Home Batteries

## Overview
EcoHome battery storage systems let you store surplus solar energy and use it when your panels aren't generating — typically in the evening or on cloudy days. Batteries can also charge from the grid during off-peak tariffs.

## Key Features
- Lithium iron phosphate (LFP) chemistry — safer and longer-lasting than standard lithium-ion
- Indoor or garage installation (IP55 rated — not suitable for outdoor exposed locations)
- Compatible with all EcoHome solar panel systems and most third-party solar installations
- Time-of-use optimisation: automatically charges from grid on cheap-rate tariffs (e.g. Octopus Go)
- Real-time monitoring via EcoHome app

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I install a battery without solar panels?**
Yes. A battery-only installation lets you charge from the grid at cheap off-peak rates and use that energy at peak times, reducing bills. However, the savings are smaller than a combined solar + battery system.

**How long will a battery power my home?**
A PowerVault 10 (10 kWh) can power typical evening usage (lighting, TV, appliances) for 8–12 hours depending on your household's consumption.

**Can I expand capacity later?**
Yes. PowerVault units are stackable. You can add a second unit at a later date.

**Does the battery provide backup power during a blackout?**
EcoHome batteries include an optional islanding/backup mode. This must be selected at the time of installation and requires a compatible gateway unit (£299).

## Safety & Compliance
- UN 38.3 transport certified
- IEC 62619 safety standard compliant
- All installations comply with BS 7671 and DNO (Distribution Network Operator) G98/G99 notification requirements

> **Important:** Battery storage must be installed by a qualified electrician. Do not attempt to install, modify, or repair battery units yourself. In case of swelling, heat, or unusual odour, evacuate and call emergency services.
