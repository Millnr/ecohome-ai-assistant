# EV Chargers

## Overview
EcoHome supplies and installs smart home EV chargepoints. All units are OZEV-approved and comply with the UK government's smart charger requirements. Our installers are registered with OZEV and can handle the grant application on your behalf.

## Product Range

### EcoHome Charge 7
- **Output:** 7.4 kW (single phase)
- **Connector:** Tethered Type 2 cable (5m or 8m) or untethered socket
- **Charging speed:** ~25–30 miles of range per hour
- **Smart features:** App scheduling, off-peak tariff optimisation, energy usage tracking
- **Installation:** Wall-mounted (garage, driveway wall, carport)
- **Warranty:** 3 years

### EcoHome Charge 22
- **Output:** 22 kW (three phase — requires 3-phase supply)
- **Connector:** Untethered Type 2 socket
- **Charging speed:** ~75–80 miles of range per hour
- **Smart features:** Full app control, load balancing, solar integration
- **Installation:** Requires three-phase electricity supply — surveyor will confirm eligibility
- **Warranty:** 3 years

## OZEV Grant — Current Eligibility (as at 2025)

> **Important:** The OZEV grant for homeowners with a driveway ended in March 2022. Do NOT tell customers this grant is still available to all homeowners.

### Who IS still eligible:
- **Renters and flat owners** (living in rental accommodation or flats/apartments) — 75% of the cost, up to **£350 per socket**
- **Landlords** (residential or commercial) — separate landlord grant, up to **£350 per socket**, max 200 sockets per application
- **Small businesses** — Workplace Charging Scheme (WCS) — up to £350 per socket, max 40 sockets

### Who is NOT eligible:
- Homeowners who own their property outright and have a driveway — no grant currently available
- Properties without off-street parking

### Grant Rules
- Charger must be a smart charger (OZEV-approved)
- **The installer applies for the grant on your behalf** — customers do not apply directly
- Charger must be installed at the property address where the applicant lives
- One grant per eligible parking space

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need off-street parking?**
Yes. A home EV charger must be installed at a fixed location — driveway, garage, or carport. On-street installation is not possible with this product.

**Can I charge any EV?**
Yes. The Type 2 connector is the UK and European standard and is compatible with all modern electric vehicles.

**How long does installation take?**
A standard domestic install takes 2–4 hours. A site survey is required first to assess cabling routes and fuse board capacity.

**Can I use my EV charger with solar panels?**
Yes. EcoHome Charge units integrate with our solar + battery systems to prioritise solar energy for EV charging during daylight hours.

**What if I live in a flat?**
You may be eligible for the OZEV grant as a renter or flat owner. Contact us to discuss your specific situation.

## Safety & Compliance
- All units comply with BS EN 61851-1 EV charging standard
- OZEV-approved smart charger (meets smart charger requirements effective June 2022)
- Installations comply with BS 7671 18th Edition wiring regulations
- Load balancing and earth fault protection included as standard

> **Important:** EV charger installation must be carried out by a qualified, OZEV-registered electrician. Do not attempt self-installation.
