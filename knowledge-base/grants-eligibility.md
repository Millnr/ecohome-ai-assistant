# Grants & Eligibility

## Overview
Several UK government schemes can help reduce the upfront cost of solar panels, batteries, EV chargers, and heat pumps. Eligibility rules differ by scheme — this document covers the main options available as of 2025/2026.

> **Important:** Grant schemes change frequently. Always confirm current eligibility directly with the relevant scheme before quoting figures to a customer.

---

## 1. Warm Homes Plan

The UK government's **Warm Homes Plan** is the primary framework for home energy upgrade support from 2025 onwards.

### Warm Homes: Local Grant (formerly ECO4 successor)
- **Who it's for:** Low-income households in England, in properties with an EPI rating of D, E, F, or G
- **What it covers:** Insulation, solar panels, heat pumps, batteries, heating upgrades
- **How much:** Full or partial grant — amount depends on household income and local authority scheme
- **How to apply:** Via your local authority or an approved scheme provider
- **Key condition:** Property must be owner-occupied or a private rented property (landlord must consent)

### Warm Homes: Low/Zero Interest Loan
- **Who it's for:** Homeowners not eligible for grant funding (higher incomes)
- **What it covers:** Solar, batteries, heat pumps, insulation, EV chargers
- **How much:** Up to approx. £15,000 loan at 0% or low interest (scheme launched 2025 — confirm current terms at gov.uk)
- **How to apply:** Via the Warm Homes Fund (gov.uk)

## 2. Energy Company Obligation (ECO4)

- **Who it's for:** Low-income or fuel-poor households receiving certain means-tested benefits (e.g. Universal Credit, Pension Credit, Child Tax Credit)
- **What it covers:** Primarily insulation and heating; solar and storage may be included depending on area
- **How much:** Fully funded installation in qualifying cases
- **How to apply:** Contact your energy supplier or a registered ECO4 installer
- **End date:** ECO4 runs until March 2026 — successor scheme to follow

---

## 3. Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS)

- **Who it's for:** Homeowners in England and Wales replacing an existing fossil fuel boiler or heating system
- **What it covers:** Air source heat pumps, ground source heat pumps, biomass boilers
- **How much:** **£7,500** grant for air/ground source heat pumps; £5,000 for biomass
- **How to apply:** Your MCS-certified installer applies on your behalf at point of sale
- **Key condition:** Property must have adequate insulation (EPC D or above recommended for heat pumps)
- **Note:** This scheme covers heat pumps — it does not cover solar panels or batteries directly

---

## 4. Smart Export Guarantee (SEG)

- **What it is:** A payment scheme where energy suppliers pay you for surplus solar electricity you export to the grid
- **Who is eligible:** Anyone with an MCS-certified solar installation (and a smart meter)
- **How much:** Rates vary by supplier — typically **1p–15p per kWh** exported. Shop around for the best rate
- **How to apply:** Register directly with your chosen SEG-licensed energy supplier after receiving your MCS certificate
- **Key condition:** You must have an MCS certificate — installations without it are not eligible

---

## 5. OZEV EV Chargepoint Grant

See [EV Chargers](ev-chargers.md) for full details. Summary:
- Homeowner driveway grant ended March 2022
- Still available for: **renters, flat owners, landlords**
- Value: **75% of cost, up to £350 per socket**
- Installer applies on your behalf

---

## 6. VAT Zero-Rate (0% VAT)

- **What it covers:** Solar panels, batteries, EV chargers, heat pumps, insulation, smart controls — installed at a domestic property
- **Rate:** 0% VAT (reduced from 20% in April 2022; extended)
- **Valid until:** Currently confirmed until **March 2027** — check gov.uk for extensions
- **Applies to:** Both supply and installation (labour included)
- **Note:** This applies automatically — you don't need to apply. Any quote from EcoHome will already reflect 0% VAT.

## 7. Scotland & Wales — Devolved Schemes

### Scotland
- **Home Energy Scotland (HES):** Free, impartial advice and cashback/loans for home energy improvements
  - Cashback up to £7,500 on eligible measures; additional loan up to £17,500
  - Apply via: **0808 808 2282** or homeenergyscotland.org

### Wales
- **Warm Homes Nest:** Free energy efficiency improvements for households on low incomes or with a qualifying health condition
  - Apply via: **0808 808 2244** or nestwales.org.uk
- **Warm Homes Optimised Retrofit (WHOPR):** For social landlords and some private tenants

## Common Edge Cases

**Can I get a grant as a landlord?**
It depends on the scheme. ECO4 and Warm Homes Local Grant can cover privately rented properties — the landlord must consent and in some cases co-fund. The OZEV EV grant has a separate landlord strand. Contact EcoHome to discuss your specific situation.

**Can I combine multiple grants?**
In some cases yes — for example, a Warm Homes loan could fund solar while the Boiler Upgrade Scheme funds a heat pump. However, some schemes prohibit double-funding of the same measure. EcoHome's advisors can help you identify the best combination.

**I already have solar — can I get a grant for a battery?**
Yes. Standalone battery installations are eligible for 0% VAT and may qualify for Warm Homes loan funding. Grant eligibility depends on your income and property rating.
